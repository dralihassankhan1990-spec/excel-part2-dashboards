# Excel for IT Project Management — Part 2: Dashboards & Reporting

**Author:** Muhammad Bilal · **Brand:** Soch & Systems — *Thinking. Technology. Transformation.*

The direct sequel to the [42-formula course](https://github.com/dralihassankhan1990-spec/excel-for-IT-project-management) — 30 teaching units that turn the same IT project dataset into a one-page project dashboard: PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and timelines, conditional formatting, KPI cards, and a print-ready weekly status report.

## What's inside
- **Workbook** — the IT PM dataset (120 work items, 4 projects) with pivot tables, charts, KPI sheets and the one-page dashboard.
- **Slides** — detailed PPT (one slide per unit) and concise PPT.
- **Guide** — Word guide with steps, examples and tips for every unit.
- **Demos** — 30 demo videos (Roman Urdu voiceover, Gemini Achird) + one combined video. Playlist link added once uploaded (Unlisted).
- **Practice website** — live at the Vercel link below: pick a unit, practise on the real dataset, check your answer instantly.

## The 30 units
**PivotTables (01–08):** first PivotTable · rows/columns/values/filters · group by Status & Priority · Show Values As · group dates by Month · Calculated Field · GETPIVOTDATA · refresh.
**PivotCharts & slicers (09–14):** PivotChart · combo chart Planned vs Actual · slicers · timeline · one slicer, two pivots · chart formatting.
**Conditional formatting & KPIs (15–20):** data bars · color scales · icon sets (RAG) · KPI cards · sparklines · Top 5 owners.
**Dashboard build (21–26):** layout · assemble · interactive dashboard · variance view · weekly status report · protect the dashboard.
**Power Query, refresh & sharing (27–30):** clean the data · Refresh All · data validation · print, PDF and share.
